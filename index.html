Who We Are

Built on Trust.Connected to the World.

From buying steel on world markets to erecting the buildings it becomes, Gillani Group Of Companies brings six specialist companies together across Pakistan, the UAE and Canada.

Gillani Enterprises began steel indenting in Pakistan in 2002. Today the group sources ferrous and non-ferrous scrap, steel and raw materials, exports through offices in Dubai and Canada, and builds pre-engineered steel buildings and infrastructure.
