Who We Are

Built on Trust.Connected to the World.

Gillani Group Of Companies started in 2002 as a steel-indenting business and has grown, one step at a time, into six specialist companies that source, trade, export and build.

Gillani Enterprises began by indenting steel and melting scrap from overseas suppliers for Pakistan’s steel mills. Local trading followed, supplying scrap, steel and metals to mills and industry across Pakistan. Export and import came next, with buyers and suppliers around the world. From there the group moved into infrastructure, with roads, water structures and housing-scheme works, and into building projects, designing and erecting pre-engineered steel buildings.

Today one group covers the whole chain: the material, the trade that moves it, and the structures it becomes.
