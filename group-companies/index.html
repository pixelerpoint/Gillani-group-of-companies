Steel Indenting & Sourcing

Gillani Enterprises

Gillani Enterprises began steel indenting in Pakistan in 2002 and is where the group started. It sources steel, scrap and metals from trusted overseas suppliers for Pakistan’s mills and manufacturers.

  • Steel indenting, from enquiry to shipment
  • Global product and supplier sourcing
  • Import, documents and customs clearance

Local Trading

Gillani Trading

Gillani Trading carries the same steel and metal products the group sources overseas, traded locally across Pakistan for mills, re-rollers and manufacturers.

  • Scrap, steel and metals traded locally
  • Supply across Pakistan
  • Inland transportation to the buyer

Pre-Engineered Steel Buildings · Joint-venture partner

ASM Buildings

ASM Buildings designs, fabricates, supplies and erects pre-engineered steel buildings, one roof to the last bolt, for industry, aviation, commerce and institutions.

  • Designs approved by five engineering bodies
