Overview

Flat steel for mills and manufacturers.

Flat steel is steel rolled into thin, wide products: sheets, strip, plate and coil. Hot rolled steel is rolled at high temperature and has a scaled surface. Cold rolled steel is rolled again at room temperature, giving a smoother finish, tighter thickness tolerances and better forming.

Gillani Group Of Companies supplies CRC sheets and steel coils to re-rollers, mills and manufacturers in Pakistan. Coils and CRC sheets have been part of our range since 2010, sourced from suppliers around the world and matched to the agreed grade, size and finish.

    Grades to JIS, EN and ASTM standards, as agreed per order.

  • International sourcing

FAQ

Questions buyers ask.

Hot rolled steel is rolled at high temperature and has a scaled, rougher surface. Cold rolled steel is rolled again at room temperature, which gives a smoother finish, tighter tolerances and better strength for forming.

CRC stands for cold rolled coil, and CRC sheets are sheets cut from it. In Pakistan the term is widely used for cold rolled carbon steel sheet.

Common standards are JIS G3141 (SPCC), EN 10130 (DC01) and ASTM A1008 for cold rolled steel, and JIS G3131 (SPHC), JIS G3101 (SS400) and ASTM A1011 for hot rolled steel. The grade is agreed per order.

Flat-rolled non-alloy steel 600 mm or wider is HS 7208 when hot rolled and HS 7209 when cold rolled. Narrower strip falls under HS 7211.
