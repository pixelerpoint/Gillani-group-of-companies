Overview

Long steel for construction and re-rolling.

Long steel products are long, narrow sections rolled from billets: reinforcing bars, wire rod, rounds, angles, channels and beams. Rebar gives concrete the tensile strength it lacks, while billets are the semi-finished feedstock that re-rolling mills reheat and roll into these products.

Gillani Group Of Companies supplies deformed rebar and steel billets, and other steel products on request, for construction and for re-rolling mills in Pakistan. We source from suppliers around the world and match each order to the agreed standard, grade and size.

    Rebar and billets matched to the agreed standard and grade.

FAQ

Questions buyers ask.

Steel bar with ribs rolled onto its surface so that it grips the surrounding concrete. It carries the tension that concrete alone cannot.

ASTM A615 Grade 60 has a minimum yield strength of 420 MPa, and BS 4449 B500B has 500 MPa. Other properties differ too, so the grade should follow the project’s design code.

Billets are semi-finished steel. Re-rolling mills reheat them and roll them into rebar, wire rod, rounds and other sections.

Common sizes are 100, 120, 130 and 150 mm square, in lengths of 6 to 12 m. Size and grade are agreed per order.

Deformed rebar is HS 7214.20. Square billets are HS 7207.11 or 7207.19 with under 0.25% carbon, and HS 7207.20 with 0.25% or more.
