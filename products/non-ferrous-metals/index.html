Overview

Non-ferrous metals for recycling and industry.

Non-ferrous metals contain little or no iron: copper, aluminium, brass and other alloys. They do not rust, conduct electricity and heat well, and can be recycled again and again without losing their properties. That makes non-ferrous scrap a valuable source of metal for refiners, smelters and manufacturers.

Gillani Group Of Companies supplies industrial non-ferrous scrap and metals: compressor scrap, motor scrap, industrial scrap, metal wires and alloys. We source from suppliers around the world and trade the material in Pakistan, with grade, preparation and packing agreed for each order.

  • Recycled metal value

    Metal recovered from scrap needs far less energy than metal made from ore.

  • International sourcing

    Material bought from suppliers around the world.

FAQ

Questions buyers ask.

Metals that contain little or no iron, such as copper, aluminium, lead, zinc and nickel, and alloys such as brass and bronze. They resist rust and keep their properties when they are recycled.

Sealed compressor units from refrigerators, air conditioners and chillers. Each unit holds copper windings and steel parts inside a welded steel shell.

Whole or dismantled electric motors, mainly copper-wound. In ISRI terms this is known as Elmo; shredded motors from ferrous shredders are known as Shelmo.

HS 7404.00 covers copper waste and scrap, including copper alloys. HS 7602.00 covers aluminium waste and scrap. The code for mixed material depends on its content.
