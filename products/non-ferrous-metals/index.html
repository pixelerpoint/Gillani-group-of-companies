Overview

Non-ferrous metals for recycling and industry.

Copper and aluminium do not rust and can be recycled again and again without losing their properties, which makes non-ferrous scrap a valuable source of metal for refiners, smelters and manufacturers.

Gillani Group Of Companies supplies compressors, motors, copper cable and aluminium alloy. We source from suppliers around the world and trade the material in Pakistan, with grade, preparation and packing agreed for each order.

  • Recycled metal value

    Metal recovered from scrap needs far less energy than metal made from ore.

  • International sourcing

    Material bought from suppliers around the world.

  • Agreed specification

    Grade, preparation and packing agreed before each order.
