Overview

Ferrous scrap for Pakistan’s steel mills.

Melting scrap for Pakistan’s steel mills and re-rolling mills. We also indent HMS, shredded and bundled scrap from suppliers around the world and trade it locally.

Gillani Group Of Companies supplies HMS, shredded and other grades, as well as bundles, to steel manufacturers and re-rolling mills in Pakistan. We source from suppliers around the world through Gillani Enterprises, Kingsbridge Metals and King Bridge Regal Trading, and trade locally through Gillani Trading.

  • Recycled feedstock

    Scrap replaces part of the raw iron ore and energy needed to make new steel.

  • International sourcing

    Material bought from suppliers around the world.

  • Local trading

    Scrap also traded within Pakistan through Gillani Trading.

Quality & Safety

Clean, safe loads, checked before they ship.

Every load must be free from

  • Radioactive material

  • Explosives

  • Arms and ammunition

  • Closed or pressurised containers

  • Gas cylinders

  • Excessive dirt, oil, non-metallic and non-ferrous material

Inspection in this trade

  • Pre-shipment inspection

    Loads are commonly inspected against the agreed grade before they ship.

  • Weight and grade checks

    Weight and grade are checked against the contract specification.

  • Radiation checks

    Radiation screening is standard practice in the scrap trade.

Inspection arrangements are agreed per order.
