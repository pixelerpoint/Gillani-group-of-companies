Overview

Ferrous scrap for Pakistan’s steel mills.

Ferrous scrap is iron and steel that has reached the end of its first use: old structures, machinery, vehicles and production offcuts. Sorted and graded, it becomes recycled feedstock for steel mills, re-rolling mills and induction and electric arc furnaces, at lower cost and with less energy than making steel from raw iron ore.

Gillani Group Of Companies supplies HMS, shredded and other grades, as well as bundles, to steel manufacturers and re-rolling mills in Pakistan. We source from suppliers around the world through Gillani Enterprises, Kingsbridge Metals and King Bridge Regal Trading, and trade locally through Gillani Trading.

  • Recycled feedstock

    Scrap replaces part of the raw iron ore and energy needed to make new steel.

  • International sourcing

    Material bought from suppliers around the world.

  • Local trading

    Scrap also traded within Pakistan through Gillani Trading.

Quality & Safety

Clean, safe loads, checked before they ship.

FAQ

Questions buyers ask.

HMS 1 (ISRI 200–202) is wrought iron and steel scrap 1/4 inch (6.35 mm) and thicker, with no galvanized or coated material. HMS 2 (ISRI 203–206) is 1/8 inch (3.175 mm) and thicker and may include black and galvanized material.

It is a blend of 80% HMS 1 and 20% HMS 2. Other common blends are 70:30 and 60:40. The ratio is agreed in each contract.

Shredded scrap (ISRI 210–211) comes from hammer-mill shredders, mainly from cars, and is magnetically separated, so it is dense and uniform. #1 Shredded has copper up to 0.20% and #2 Shredded up to 0.30%. HMS is wrought iron and steel scrap graded by thickness and piece size.

The minimum order is discussed per order. Contact us with the grade and quantity you need.

Shipping and trade documents are provided as agreed per contract.
