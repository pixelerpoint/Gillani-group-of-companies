Overview

Steel buildings, designed and built in-house.

A pre-engineered steel building is designed as one system: primary frames, secondary members, roof and wall cladding and accessories, fabricated in the factory and bolted together on site. It goes up faster than a conventional building and can span large, column-free spaces.

ASM Buildings handles every stage, from design and fabrication to supply and erection. Designs follow current American codes using Tekla and ETABS, and have been approved by five engineering bodies: NESPAK, MES, DW&CE, AHR Global UK and UET Peshawar.

  • Designs to code

    Current American codes, with Tekla and ETABS.

  • Quality assured

    QA and QC policy across design and fabrication.

  • Safety first

    Safety first on every installation.
