Overview

Connecting Pakistan’s industry with world markets.

International trade links buyers and suppliers across borders. A trading partner that knows both sides can match material to the buyer’s specification, agree clear terms and keep the shipment and paperwork on track.

Two group companies handle export and international trade: King Bridge Regal Trading L.L.C. for export and trading, and Kingsbridge Metals for import and trading. Together they trade metals with buyers and suppliers around the world, including Pakistan’s steel mills.

  • Two trading companies

    King Bridge Regal Trading L.L.C. and Kingsbridge Metals.

  • Global reach

    Suppliers across Europe, the Middle East, the Americas and Asia.

  • Transparent terms

    Clear pricing, terms and documents, every time.
