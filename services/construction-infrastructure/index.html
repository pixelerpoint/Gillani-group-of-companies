Overview

Infrastructure for growing communities.

Housing schemes and towns need the works underneath them: roads, earthworks, culverts and water structures, sewerage and power. Each has to be built to specification, checked as it goes and finished on time.

Touheed Enterprises is PEC licensed in category C-3 for 2024–25 and 2025–26 and works as a DHA Islamabad–Rawalpindi contractor. Its teams handle roads and earthworks, water structures, housing-scheme works, electrification and heavy-equipment rental.
