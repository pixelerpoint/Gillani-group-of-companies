Overview

Steel and metals, traded close to home.

Local trading lets mills and manufacturers buy material that is already in Pakistan, in the quantities they need, without waiting for an overseas shipment or managing an import themselves.

Gillani Trading carries the same steel and metal products the group sources overseas, traded locally across Pakistan. From our head office in Lahore we work closely with the mills and manufacturers we supply across Pakistan.

    The same steel and metal products the group imports.
