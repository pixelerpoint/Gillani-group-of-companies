Who We Are

Six companies. One steel chain.

From buying steel on world markets to erecting the buildings it becomes, Gillani Group Of Companies brings six specialist companies together.

The group began in 2002 as Gillani Enterprises, a steel indenting and international trading business in Pakistan. Today it supplies ferrous and non-ferrous metals, steel and raw materials to Pakistan’s mills and manufacturers, trades metals with buyers and suppliers around the world, and builds pre-engineered steel buildings and infrastructure through its partner companies.

  • Source & trade

    Scrap, steel and metals sourced and traded for Pakistan’s industry.

  • Export & import

    Metals traded with buyers and suppliers around the world.

  • Build

    Pre-engineered steel buildings and infrastructure.

Chairman’s Message

Chairman’s message

Trust is built one shipment, one structure and one promise at a time.

We began trading steel in 2002 to give Pakistan’s mills reliable access to the material they need, on fair terms. Today that aim has grown into a group that sources, trades, exports and builds, across three countries.

Our clients ask for clear specifications, honest dealing and delivery as promised. That is the standard we hold ourselves to, and the reason clients stay with us for the long term.
