About Gillani Group Of Companies

Steel and metal sourcing, trading and construction, since 2002.

Who We Are

Built on trust. Connected to the world.

Gillani Group Of Companies began in 2002 as Gillani Enterprises, a steel indenting and international trading business in Pakistan. It has since grown into six companies that source, trade, export and build.

We supply ferrous and non-ferrous metals, steel and raw materials to industry in Pakistan, export and import with buyers and suppliers around the world, and deliver steel buildings and construction through our joint-venture partners.

  • Since 2002Steel trading in Pakistan
  • 50+Countries in our network
  • 50+Steel and metal products
